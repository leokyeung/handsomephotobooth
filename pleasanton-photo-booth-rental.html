Firsthand experience at Palm Event Center & Casa Real

Wine Country estate receptions have a very different feel—and very different lighting—from bright daytime rooms. Having worked weddings repeatedly at both Palm Event Center in the Vineyard and Casa Real at Ruby Hill Winery, we plan our setup around how those spaces actually flow during cocktail hour, dinner, and dancing:

Casa Real at Ruby Hill Winery

Casa Real receptions shine with warm architectural uplighting, high ceilings, and lively Grand Salon dance floors. Tablet booths struggle with grain in warm evening lighting—our DSLR camera and diffused studio flash keep skin tones flattering, formalwear sharp, and prints vibrant.

Our open-air layout easily fits full bridal-party and table group shots, and our custom templates pair naturally with romantic winery florals, monograms, and timeless black-and-white or full-color keepsakes.

Palm Event Center in the Vineyard

Inside Palm Event Center’s Estate Room, dramatic chandeliers and candlelight create an intimate evening atmosphere while guests move smoothly between the bar, lounge spaces, and dance floor.

We coordinate load-in ahead of guest arrival, position our 10 ft × 10 ft setup so the queue never blocks catering pathways, and keep prints drying instantly for guests and optional $99 scrapbook signing.

Celebrating elsewhere in the Tri-Valley? We also bring the booth to Ruby Hill golf and country club events, downtown Pleasanton celebrations, Livermore Valley wineries, and corporate gatherings across Pleasanton, Dublin, and San Ramon.
