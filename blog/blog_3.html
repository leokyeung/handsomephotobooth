How Much Does a Wedding Photo Booth Cost in the Bay Area?

Updated October 9, 2026 · Handsome Photobooth

Handsome Photobooth’s published three-hour Glam Package is $899. It includes DSLR photos, unlimited instant prints, a custom template, props, a backdrop, studio lighting, an attendant, QR downloads, and a digital album. That is a concrete starting point for your wedding budget, rather than an estimate of what every Bay Area vendor charges.

The final cost depends on the package, hours, optional extras, and venue logistics. Below is a breakdown of our published offering and a checklist for comparing quotes on equal terms. Confirm your date and venue with us for a final written quote.

What does the $899 wedding photo booth package include?

  • Three operating hours: time when guests can use the booth. Ask how the operating window fits your dinner, speeches, and dancing.
  • DSLR photography and studio lighting: an open-air setup with a modern backdrop.
  • Unlimited instant prints: choose a 2 × 6 photo strip or 4 × 6 format.
  • A personalized print template: designed around your celebration’s names, date, colors, and style.
  • Props and a professional attendant: someone to assist guests throughout the rental.
  • Digital copies: QR downloads and a downloadable Google Photos album.

Setup and breakdown are included in the package described on our website. Review the Bay Area wedding photo booth package for the current details and contact us to confirm travel and access arrangements for your venue.

Why quotes from different vendors vary

A digital-only booth, a booth with instant prints, and a 360 video experience solve different needs. Compare what guests actually receive, not just the headline price. Ask to see sample photos taken with the offered equipment and examples of the print format included in the quote.

Duration also matters. A lower price for two hours may not cover the reception window you want. An unattended rental may require more coordination from your planner. Neither option is automatically wrong; the useful question is whether it fits your celebration. Our open-air versus enclosed booth guide explains the layout differences.

Questions to ask before signing

  1. What is the final total? Ask whether taxes, travel, parking, bridge tolls, and any venue-specific access costs are included or separate.
  2. What counts as an operating hour? If you want a break during dinner, confirm whether idle time costs extra and how the start and end times are recorded.
  3. When can the team load in? Share stairs, elevators, loading restrictions, and early setup requests before the quote is finalized.
  4. How do prints work? Confirm the format, reprint policy, and whether an album or scrapbook is included.
  5. What happens if plans change? Read the deposit, payment, cancellation, and rescheduling terms in the agreement.

Choose hours that guests can use

A longer rental is useful only if it matches the evening. Place the booth near the reception activity while leaving room for a queue. Plan around dinner and formalities, and have your planner announce when the booth opens. Read our wedding photo booth timeline guide before adding hours.

You can also send your coordinator our space, power, and setup checklist. Resolving those details early makes quotes easier to compare and avoids last-minute layout changes.
